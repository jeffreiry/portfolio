**About the Role**

This is the founding product design role at an early-stage B2B SaaS company building an agentic integration platform. You will own design end-to-end, working directly with the Chief Product Officer, and you will also ship the production frontend yourself.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Design the agentic UX where users and coding agents collaborate to build enterprise integrations together.
- Design the platform UX covering deploys, runtime, observability, and logs for the cloud environment where integrations run.
- Implement and ship production frontend code in React and TypeScript directly, with no handoffs to engineers.
- Establish the design language, component system, and UX patterns for the product from the ground up.

**Required Qualifications**

- 1 to 5 years of product design experience with a portfolio of shipped work demonstrating exceptional craft and taste, particularly for AI-native or technical B2B SaaS products.
- Proven ability to implement and ship production frontend code in React and TypeScript without handoffs.
- Experience designing dense, technical B2B SaaS products with a strong understanding of agentic or human-computer interaction patterns.
- Experience establishing design systems, component libraries, or design language standards.
- Proficiency in high-fidelity design and prototyping tools such as Figma or equivalent.
- Comfort working in a fast-paced, high-intensity, in-person early-stage environment.

Location: San Francisco, CA. Salary range: $150,000 - $250,000.
