Hi, we're Oscar. Oscar is the first health insurance company built around a full stack technology platform and a relentless focus on serving our members. We started Oscar in 2012 to create the kind of health insurance company we would want for ourselves—one that behaves like a doctor in the family.

**About the role**

As a Product Designer for the Lucie Marketplace, you will design and ship the tools that help employers easily manage health plan contributions for their employees. The Lucie ecosystem spans the direct enrollment platform for brokers, the consumer marketplace, and ICHRA tooling, with this role focusing on shaping the ICHRA experience while contributing to other areas as needed.

**Key Requirements**

The ideal candidate brings a strong visual design portfolio and a proven track record of designing complex enterprise software, data-intensive platforms, or workforce management tools. You should be able to work autonomously across multiple technical domains from initial research through high-fidelity delivery, consistently balancing execution speed with deep user empathy.

**Required Skills & Tools**

You should thrive in a modern workflow and be comfortable leveraging AI-assisted tools like Claude Code alongside Figma to streamline the design-to-engineering process.

**Collaboration**

You will collaborate cross-functionally with product managers, engineers, research, data and operational stakeholders to take features from discovery through delivery while driving a consistent, scalable, and intuitive experience across the entire Lucie suite.
