## Job description original

Join the company that's building the telemetry infrastructure for the AI era. At Cribl, we partner with IT and Security teams at many of the world's biggest enterprises, including half of the Fortune 100.

**Why You'll Love This Role**

We are looking for a Senior Product Designer to help define and deliver Cribl security product experience. You will design for security practitioners across detection, alerting, investigation, triage, search, threat coverage, telemetry, and AI-assisted workflows.

This role requires a genuine security background and the ability to turn complex SOC workflows into clear, trustworthy experiences.

**As An Active Member Of Our Team, You Will…**

- Lead end-to-end design from problem framing and research through interaction design, prototyping, validation, and implementation.
- Design workflows for detection creation and management, alert review, investigation, triage, search pivots, threat coverage, normalization, and enrichment.
- Apply security judgment to evidence, provenance, confidence, severity, permissions, auditability, destructive actions, and operational risk.
- Design AI-assisted and agentic experiences that make intent, context, sources, capabilities, uncertainty, and user approval clear.
- Use Cribl's design system and contribute reusable components, patterns, guidance, and interaction models.
- Work expertly in Figma for exploration, prototyping, documentation, collaboration, and handoff.

**Required:**

- 5+ years experience designing complex B2B software, enterprise platforms, or products for technical users.
- A security background, such as security product design, security operations, threat detection, incident response, threat hunting, detection engineering, SOC workflows, SIEM, EDR, SOAR, or a related discipline.
- Understanding of security practitioners' realities, including noisy signals, incomplete evidence, time pressure, and high-consequence decisions.
- Strong product thinking and interaction-design skills.
- Experience designing AI-assisted, agentic, conversational, or other emerging interaction models.
- Expert Figma skills, including advanced prototyping and design documentation.
- Demonstrated expertise working in and contributing to design systems.
- Understanding of AI tools and AI UX interaction models is required.
- A portfolio with case studies that show your role, process, decisions, collaboration, and outcomes.

**Highly desired:** Dovetail, security data/detections/alerts/threat intelligence, designing for roles/permissions/tenants.

**A plus:** Accessible product design, WCAG, OCSF/MITRE ATT&CK/KQL/Sigma.
