Project-based | Remote | United States | Contract. Pay based on freelancer's rate.

Control Shift Video is looking for a freelance designer to collaborate with our creative team on project-based work across video, motion graphics, web visuals, and brand content. Our projects span technology, healthcare, energy, consumer brands, nonprofits, and passion projects.

- Design visual systems, frames, and assets for video and motion projects.

- Translate concepts into clean, purposeful designs.
- Prepare files for animation and production workflows.

**What We're Looking For**

- Strong design fundamentals and visual judgment.
- Experience designing for motion or video.
- Clear communication and reliable delivery.
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite or similar tools.

**How We Work**

- Remote-first and flexible. Project-based engagements with clear scopes and timelines.
