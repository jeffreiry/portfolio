## Job description original

**Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human** Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era.

We are looking for a talented Senior Product Designer who can help shape Okta's expansive product portfolio by bringing exceptional craft and fresh perspective to the world of identity and security.

**Finer print**

You will be designing the user experience for **Okta Privileged Access**, one of the fastest-moving products at Okta. We provide unified access and governance for privileged resources — servers, service accounts, and secrets, whether on-prem or cloud — without compromising user experience. Our users are IT and security admins, infrastructure engineers, and developers who need access to production.

**What you will do**

- Drive and execute an ambitious product design strategy for your team.
- Bring a systems-thinking lens to connect user insights and business goals.
- Drive impact by framing problems and exploring solutions in collaboration with user research, UX writing, product management, engineering, marketing, and data science.
- Design end-to-end flows that involve multiple personas – IT Admin, Security admins, End-user.
- Perfect the details to design scalable and usable design patterns.
- Regularly present work to cross-functional team members and senior leadership.
- Advocate and encourage thoughtful use of AI for brainstorming and iterating on potential solutions.

**What we are looking for**

- You easily move between big picture thinking and being detail-oriented.
- You're curious and have experience incorporating AI into your design process.
- Evaluate AI-generated outputs for technical accuracy, design system adherence, and ease of use.
- You keep user goals at the heart of your solution and can also balance business objectives and engineering considerations.
- You thrive in ambiguous environments and know when to lead versus when to lean on your team members.
- You build and share empathy with people.
- You are someone who sees design as a partner with PM and Engineering.
- You are comfortable owning all stages of design for multiple teams within a given product area.
- You showcase storytelling skills to communicate your design ideas.
- Your portfolio will show that you've worked on complex and challenging technical projects across all aspects of the design process.

**Qualifications**

- Degree in a related field or relevant work experience as a Product Designer or UX Designer.
- Proficient in Figma or similar design tools.
- At least a basic understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- A digital portfolio showing depth across a variety of projects, including work that shipped and was used in real products.
- 5+ years of experience as Product Designer

The annual base salary range for this position for candidates located in Canada is between: $118,000—$162,000 CAD
