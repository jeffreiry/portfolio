Sidecar's ride marketplace connects everyday drivers in their own car with people nearby for safe and affordable rides, allowing people to choose a ride based on vehicle, price and ETA using their smartphone.

**Key Requirements**

The position requires an exceptional portfolio of shipped creative product design showcasing interaction and visual design talents, mastery of Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, etc.), ability to self-manage multiple projects and deadlines, and experience shipping software preferably at a well-known software organization. A BA/BFA in interactive design or related discipline and/or 5+ years of relevant experience is needed.

**Role Focus**

The role focuses on user interaction and visual design within a mobility platform, involving research, prototyping, and collaboration with various teams to create beautiful and functional visuals for a suite of mobile products.

**Compensation & Benefits:** Full-time salary negotiable based on experience, stock options, health/dental/vision coverage, unlimited vacation policy.
