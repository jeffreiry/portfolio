Leopard is an insurtech company in the Financial Services (Life Insurance, Annuities) industry, incubated by The D. E. Shaw Group's venture studio, now integrated into Coventry.

**Role Responsibilities**

The role involves spearheading the end-to-end design of user experiences for both internal business operations tools and external consumer-facing platforms within the life insurance and annuity sector. Designers develop and maintain a robust design system to ensure consistency, accessibility, and scalability across all product surfaces.

**Key Requirements**

Portfolio requirements include end-to-end design projects for both B2B and B2C products with detailed case studies demonstrating your process for defining information architecture, conducting user research, and developing user flows. Examples should demonstrate how you've designed for complex, high-stakes decisions, highlighting empathy and user-centric problem-solving.

**AI-Focused Skills**

The role involves driving the design and implementation of AI-native features, focusing on intuitive user interaction with intelligent guidance, dynamic recommendations, and agent-driven workflows.

**Experience Level:** 3-7 years (mid-senior) to 5-10 years (senior), salary $130k-$180k USD annually.
