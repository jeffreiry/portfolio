## Job description original

It all started when engineer Fred Luddy wrote code that automated a tedious task for his coworker, Phyllis. Today, ServiceNow is the AI control tower for business reinvention.

As part of the Spark Team, you will operate as an IC3 Senior Demo Designer: a strong independent contributor who delivers high-quality design work within defined project areas. You will use advanced Figma craft, prototyping, and visual storytelling to turn early product ideas into clear, compelling concepts that support team alignment, customer understanding, and leadership communication.

**What you get to do in this role:**

- Deliver independently within defined scope by taking assigned innovation concepts, demo moments, or product stories from rough direction to polished design artifacts.
- Demonstrate strong Figma craft by creating component-based designs, auto layout structures, variants, clickable prototypes.
- Translate product ideas into clear stories by shaping customer needs, product direction, and experience goals into simple narratives, demo flows, storyboards.
- Build high-fidelity prototypes using Figma, AI-powered prototyping tools, and code-assisted workflows.
- Create proof-of-concepts that help evaluate emerging AI experiences and future product directions.
- Communicate design decisions clearly in critiques, working sessions, and leadership reviews.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

- 5+ years of product design, visual design, interaction design, innovation design, or related experience.
- Proven ability to operate as a strong independent contributor.
- Advanced Figma skills, including component-based design, auto layout, variants, prototyping.
- Strong storytelling ability translating customer needs into narratives, demo flows, leadership-ready presentations.
- Experience with AI-assisted prototyping workflows.
- Experience building prototypes using code-assisted tools.
- Familiarity with design systems and component-based architecture.
- Strong visual and interaction design fundamentals, including motion-aware design thinking.
- Working knowledge of motion, video, animation, 3D, or emerging AI-assisted design workflows is a plus.

**Basic Qualifications:**

- BA, BFA, or equivalent practical experience.
- Strong proficiency with Figma and modern digital design workflows.

For positions in this location, we offer a base pay of $143,200 - $243,400.
