## Job description original

Join us in building the future of finance. Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades.

**About the team + role**

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. The Design team's mission is to design best-in-class financial experiences that empower users to manage their financial lives with confidence.

As a Product Designer, you'll play a central role in shaping the visual and interaction design across Robinhood's product ecosystem. You'll work from 0 to 1, building entirely new systems while maintaining a cohesive design identity.

Design at Robinhood is AI-first. This is a role for someone who already uses AI as a core part of how they think, explore, and execute.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA and New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

**What you'll do**

- Use AI to explore problem spaces, surface edge cases, stress-test ideas, and generate early prototypes. Let AI accelerate the work while you guide intent, structure, and quality.
- Drive end-to-end design of key features across our products and experiences.
- Create visually compelling, cohesive designs that elevate our user experience.
- Collaborate closely with cross-functional partners including Product, Engineering, Content, and Research.
- Contribute to and evolve our design system.
- Help set the standard for design craft and product excellence across the team.

**What you bring**

- 3+ years of experience as a product designer with a strong visual design portfolio.
- Demonstrated success designing consumer-facing products, ideally in fintech or adjacent spaces.
- Experience taking products from concept to launch.
- Active use of AI in your design workflow - you already use LLMs to think through problems, explore approaches, and move faster.
- Strong systems thinking and the ability to build and maintain scalable design systems.
- Bonus: Prototyping and motion design skills to bring interactions to life.

Base Pay Range: Zone 1 (Menlo Park, CA; New York, NY): $140,000—$165,000 USD

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**Produto consumer-facing em fintech**

A vaga pede especificamente "demonstrated success designing consumer-facing products, ideally in fintech" — e o produto da Robinhood é literalmente investimentos pra consumidor final. Nenhum case do portfolio toca produto financeiro consumer; o mais próximo é o Gerenciador de Verbas, que é orçamento corporativo B2B interno, não dinheiro/investimento de pessoa física. É o mesmo tipo de gap de domínio identificado em outras vagas fintech do bench (Affirm, Caju).

**IA como ferramenta de pensamento no processo de design**

"Design at Robinhood is AI-first" não é um detalhe — é como a vaga se descreve. O requisito pede uso ativo de LLMs pra "pensar problemas, explorar abordagens e ganhar velocidade", diferente de Enterprise AI, que é sobre desenhar a experiência de um produto de IA. **Mitigação:** mesma recomendação recorrente — se Jeferson já usa ferramentas de IA generativa no processo pessoal de design, esse é o momento de nomear isso explicitamente e com detalhe, já que aparece como filtro central (não só um item de lista) nesta vaga específica.
