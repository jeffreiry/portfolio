## Job description original

Experian is a global data and technology company that powers opportunities for people and businesses around the world. We operate in diverse markets, such as financial services, healthcare, automotive, agribusiness, insurance, among others.

We are looking for a **UI Designer** with excellent visual skills, structured thinking, and mature technical knowledge to work closely with the front-end development team, creating and evolving pages and digital experiences for Marketing initiatives.

**Responsibilities:**

- Create and improve layouts using Figma, CMS platform, HTML, and CSS.
- Create and evolve pages for different Marketing initiatives and needs.
- Visually improve existing pages in terms of visual aspect, navigability, and organization.
- Deliver visual proposals aligned with the needs of each initiative.
- Work together with the technical team, understanding the limitations and possibilities of front-end.
- Ensure visual consistency, responsiveness, and application of UI best practices.
- Work with a personalization tool (training will be provided).
- Participate in discussions about user experience.

**Requirements:**

- Experience as a UI Designer.
- Mastery of Figma.
- Mastery of CSS for building and adjusting layouts.
- Ability to transform simple bases into professional interfaces.
- Understanding of the front-end technical context (even without being a dev per se).
- Clear communication, collaborative attitude, and transparency.
- Interest in learning new tools and stacks.
- Visual organization and attention to detail.
- Interdisciplinarity between Design and development.

Location: São Paulo, Brazil (hybrid).
