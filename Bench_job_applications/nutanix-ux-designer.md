## Job description original

As part of the Global Design organization, the design team is seeking a designer for our Bangalore office. We are looking for someone who is skilled at solving design problems in a highly complex domain. Nutanix has a design-first philosophy that brings consumer-grade design and web-scale engineering together.

**Your Role**

- Ramp up quickly on the Nutanix product domain, processes and culture.
- Take ownership of features or product areas and work collaboratively with PM, Engineering and other stakeholders.
- Understand problems with medium complexity and create simple and elegant design solutions.
- Produce high-quality UX designs based on first principles and data.
- Present designs to key stakeholders with the rationale for design decisions.
- Plan, conduct, document and present research work related to their product areas.

**What You Will Bring**

- Strong interaction design skills with an ability to look beyond pixels.
- Have an ability to challenge the status quo, focus conversations on the user, and produce UX designs with attention to detail.
- Work effectively in a fast-moving environment, juggle multiple projects, and prioritize work.
- Strong presentation and communication skills.
- Bias towards being organized and thinking about repeatability and scale.

**Minimum qualifications:**

- Have a strong UX design portfolio showing examples of consumer-grade design of products in complex domains.
- Proficiency with Figma and/or Sketch.
- Familiarity with user research & usability testing techniques.
- Experience working across multiple phases of design (interaction, visual design, and prototyping).
- 5+ years of experience in user experience design for web applications with a Bachelor's degree in Interaction/UX design.

**Preferred qualifications:**

- Demonstrated proficiency in UX research, design sprints/workshops, visual design/design systems, prototyping/UI engineering.
- Experience working on B2C/consumer products.
- Working knowledge of accessibility guidelines and best practices.
- Working knowledge of AI tools for design: HTML5, JavaScript and CSS3.

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**Portfólio "consumer-grade" vs. experiência inteiramente B2B enterprise**

A vaga pede explicitamente "consumer-grade design of products in complex domains" como requisito mínimo do portfólio. O portfolio de Jeferson é consistentemente B2B enterprise (Enterprise AI, Shipping Platform, Gerenciador de Verbas) ou de marca (Arezzo, Del Valle) — nunca um produto consumer direto. A complexidade de domínio está presente, mas a natureza "consumer-grade" (polimento voltado a usuário final de massa) não é o que esses cases demonstram. **Mitigação:** o argumento possível é reposicionar a narrativa dos cases enterprise em torno do rigor de craft aplicado (handoff detalhado do Arezzo), já que a vaga valoriza isso mesmo em contexto B2B — mas não resolve completamente a ausência de experiência consumer.
