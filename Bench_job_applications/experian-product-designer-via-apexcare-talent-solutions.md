**Role Summary**

The role involves spearheading the design of end-to-end user experiences for consumer-facing digital financial wellness and identity protection products, as well as developer tools.

**Key Responsibilities**

The position includes driving the creation and refinement of responsive websites and native mobile applications, ensuring a cohesive and engaging user journey. You'll collaborate closely with Product Managers, Developers, Analysts, and Marketers to translate business objectives into intuitive and effective design solutions.

Specific tasks include designing across web and mobile platforms to ensure consistency, engagement, and usability across Experian's digital services. You'll create user-centered design assets (journeys, wireframes, prototypes) informed by research, usability testing, and iterative feedback.

**Qualifications**

A Bachelor's degree in UX, Visual Design, or related field, or equivalent practical experience is required.
