## Job description original

**About Checkr**Checkr is building the data platform to power safe and fair decisions. Over 140,000 companies and millions of people rely on Checkr for AI verification in the moments that matter most: getting a new job, a new place to live, a car ride, childcare, even a date. Customers include Uber, Pennymac, Airbnb, Doordash, and Anthropic.

**About the role**

We're looking for a **Staff Product Designer** to join our Growth team and work with us to achieve our ambitious goals from customer acquisition to conversion and becoming happy customers. Our growth strategy is based on a self-serve, product-led approach, and this role is an essential part of it.

**What you'll do**

- Embed with the Growth team; help define the roadmap, vision, and lead design work.
- Own the design work for funnel optimization, NUX, and personalization for guided flows.
- Leverage AI to create personalized customer experiences.
- Design growth systems, experiments, and new product features, translating quantitative and qualitative insights into testable ideas.
- Champion designs that deliver a seamless, easy, delightful experience for our SMB users.
- Facilitate workshops that help you and your team develop winning ideas.
- Distill complex information into simple and beautiful user experiences, from brainstorming to delivering high-fidelity designs.
- Build strong relationships with interdisciplinary teams to conceptualize, build, and ship user-friendly and business-driven products.
- Participate in UX research, both with and without a UX Researcher, apply insights to designs, represent the voice of the customer, and be an advocate for users.
- Be a strong voice in sharing ideas, managing feedback from stakeholders, challenging assumptions, and bringing best practices to the team.

**What you bring**

- Your portfolio showcases high-quality work directly impacting project success
- You have 7+ years of professional design experience, including 4 years on Growth teams or in ecommerce and merchandising, where your work has been key to driving gains in conversion, basket price and product adoption.
- You've excelled on onboarding & merchandising surfaces and are comfortable with iterative experimentation like A/B testing. You also know when to lobby for a broader strategic approach
- You love uncovering insights firsthand and you're comfortable with identifying and conducting UX research and testing independently. You're innately curious and always looking to get to the heart of a problem
- You excel in complexity and fast-paced environments and are passionate about balancing craft, critical thinking, and business needs to deliver a holistic user experience.
- You're focused on psychology and why people behave the way they do. Experience with behavioral science is a plus!
- Always connecting your work to the bigger picture, you can describe high-level team goals and align projects accordingly. You skillfully use design explorations, research, documentation, and presentations to rally the team and communicate with leadership.
- You have a deep understanding of best practices and contemporary design patterns.
- Nice to have: Experience with B2B companies focusing on SMBs
- An A-player mindset with a strong bias for action: you raise the bar, move with urgency, stay resilient through ambiguity, and take ownership to deliver meaningful outcomes.

On-target Earnings OR Base Salary range (San Francisco, CA): $210,000—$247,000 USD
On-target Earnings OR Base Salary range (Denver, CO): $178,000—$209,000 USD

At Checkr, we believe an in office work environment strengthens collaboration, drives innovation, and encourages connection. Our hub locations are Denver, CO; San Francisco, CA; Nashville, TN; and Santiago, Chile. Individuals are expected to work from the office 3+ days a week.

## Gaps prioritários

**Ausência total de experiência em Growth/e-commerce com métrica de conversão**

Este é o requisito mais específico e mais central da vaga — não é um gap de competência de design genérica, é a ausência completa do domínio que a vaga existe para cobrir. A Checkr pede 4+ anos especificamente em times de Growth ou e-commerce/merchandising, com resultado comprovado em conversão, ticket médio e adoção. Nenhum case do portfolio de Jeferson passou por esse domínio — os cases mais próximos (Gerenciador de Verbas, Arezzo) são ferramentas B2B internas, não produtos de growth self-serve com funil de conversão. Some-se a isso a ausência estrutural de métricas de impacto em todo o portfolio (documentada como gap conhecido em Arezzo/Hypera: "não medimos nada depois do lançamento") — para uma vaga cujo mandato inteiro é "funnel optimization" e "gains in conversion", essa combinação de ausência de domínio + ausência de métrica é um bloqueador duplo, não mitigável com argumento de transferência. **Mitigação:** não há caminho rápido aqui — seria necessário experiência real em growth/e-commerce para essa candidatura fazer sentido.
