## Job description original

EvolutionIQ's mission is to deliver state of the art technology that helps insurance claims teams make claims handling more accurate, fair, and efficient.

**About You:** An experienced Staff Product Designer who is energized by ambiguity and by shaping entirely new product experiences around emerging technology within a complex problems space such as insurance claims management. You are comfortable defining how customers may interact with agentic products; what the system should automate, explain, or leave under human control, and how the product earns trust.

**In this Role, You Will:**

- Lead the strategic vision and conceptual design outcomes which provide a clear direction for our customer experiences across the AI landscape
- Design human and AI agent interactions, including how the system communicates uncertainty, automation, control, intervention, and recovery
- Define the experience model: conceptual model, information architecture, core workflows, interaction patterns, and guiding principles, and build the reusable agentic patterns and design-system foundations that let it scale
- Conduct discovery directly with clients to inform experience decisions, and validate concepts through rapid prototyping and usability testing
- Establish a scalable experience architecture across a complex, evolving platform
- Partner with leadership, stakeholders, cross-functional team members to shape the product, navigating tradeoffs across user needs, AI/ML capabilities, technical feasibility, and regulatory requirements

**What You Will Need:**

- 10+ years designing digital products, including leading 0-to-1 and emerging-technology products in B2B enterprise software and highly regulated or complex problem spaces
- Demonstrated experience designing AI/ML or data-driven products, with genuine fluency in how AI systems behave and how that shapes product direction; including agentic or human-in-the-loop experiences
- Exceptional systems thinking: you see patterns, anticipate edge cases, design for scale, and establish reusable frameworks and standards
- Proven ability to set experience strategy and bring structure to ambiguous, business-critical problems with little precedent
- Experience in end-to-end UX/product design to design impactful solutions to challenging problems
- Exceptional communication skills: you can articulate design decisions using product thinking, frameworks, research, and data to influence direction with cross-functional partners, executives, and clients
- Raise the bar for the people around you, as an individual contributor, through your work and your feedback.

The base salary range is $210 - $230K, with flexibility depending on a candidate's background and experience.

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**10+ anos de experiência vs. ~3-4 anos documentados**

É uma exigência explícita e numérica alta, incomum mesmo pra nível Staff — e a trajetória pública de Jeferson é consideravelmente mais curta. Mesmo com Enterprise AI sendo um fit temático quase perfeito (IA agêntica, confiança, domínio B2B complexo), a diferença de anos de experiência pode ser um filtro eliminatório na triagem inicial, independente da qualidade do case. **Mitigação:** não há forma de simular anos de experiência — essa vaga é mais apropriada pra quando a trajetória de Jeferson atingir essa marca, ou se houver experiência profissional anterior a 2022 que amplie o tempo documentado.

**Comunicação direta com executivos e clientes externos**

A vaga pede capacidade de influenciar "cross-functional partners, executives, and clients" — nenhum case do portfolio documenta esse tipo de interação direta com liderança executiva ou cliente externo (a colaboração documentada é com produto, engenharia e dados internos).
