## Job description original

**Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human** Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era.

We are looking for a talented Staff Product Designer who can help shape Okta's expansive product portfolio by bringing exceptional craft and fresh perspective to the world of identity and security.

**Finer print**

You will be designing the user experience for **Okta Identity Governance**. Large companies need to know who should have access to what and prove it to auditors. We build the tools for requesting, reviewing, and managing that access. At our customers' scale, one review can involve the whole company. Making that feel simple and intuitive is your job.

**What you will do**

- Own the long-term design strategy for your product area, shaping where it goes across multiple releases.
- Partner with design leadership to define the patterns and standards other designers build on, and raise the quality bar in your area.
- Lead through influence, aligning product, engineering, research, and content partners around a direction without relying on authority.
- Design end-to-end journeys that span the Okta portfolio and multiple personas: IT and security admins, compliance owners, resource owners, approving managers, and end users.
- Bring a systems-thinking lens, connecting user insights to business goals.
- Facilitate co-creation workshops with Product Management and Engineering.
- Mentor senior designers by guiding them on their projects and supporting their growth and career development.
- Present ideas and concepts to adjacent teams and senior leadership to drive alignment.
- Advocate and encourage thoughtful use of AI for researching, brainstorming, iterating, prototyping, and collaborating.

**What we are looking for**

- Experience leading cross-discipline teams, design as a partner with PM and Engineering.
- Move easily between big-picture thinking and detail, create clarity in ambiguity for your team and partners.
- You have the heart of a teacher and can help mentor and train people.
- You are a thought leader, undertaking explorations into new technologies while also delivering practical designs.
- You work AI into your design process — building tools and reusable prompts — and you vet its output for accuracy, design system fit, and usability before it ships.
- You keep user goals at the heart of your solutions.
- You are comfortable owning all stages of design within a given product area.
- You showcase storytelling skills.
- Your portfolio will show that you've worked on complex and challenging technical projects across all aspects of the design process (generative and evaluative research, discovery, interaction design, information architecture, visual design, etc.)

**Qualifications**

- Degree in a related field or relevant work experience as a Product Designer or UX Designer.
- Proficient in Figma or similar design tools.
- At least a basic understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- A digital portfolio showing depth across a variety of projects, including work that shipped and was used in real products.
- 7+ years of experience as Product Designer

**This person must attend in person orientation the first week of employment in either San Francisco or Chicago. This person must go into the office 2 days a week.

The annual base salary range for this position is between: $156,000—$215,000 USD
