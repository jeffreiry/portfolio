**About Understood**

Understood is a nonprofit helping millions of people navigate the realities of neurodivergence. We translate trusted knowledge about ADHD, dyslexia, and dyscalculia into clear guidance, practical tools, and community support — so people don't just feel informed, they feel understood.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Synthesizing research and user insights to identify product opportunities.
- Designing experiences for diverse users and cognitive needs.
- Thinking systemically across product experiences.
- Moving concepts through prototyping to launch while exploring AI applications in design practice.

**Required Skills & Qualifications**

- Systems thinking, user research synthesis, interaction and UX design.
- Knowledge of inclusive and accessible design principles.
- Prototyping and testing capabilities.
- Experience applying AI to design practice.
- Experienced product designer with demonstrated ability to handle complexity and ambiguity.
- Deep understanding of how people learn and make decisions.
- Commitment to inclusive design practices.

**Core Values:** Continuously learn, Act with intention, Champion difference, Inspire change, Grow together.

Location: New York, NY (in-person). Compensation: $170K–$190K/yr.
