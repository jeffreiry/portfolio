## Job description original

**Secure Every Identity, from AI to Human** Identity is the key to unlocking the potential of AI. Okta secures AI by building the trusted, neutral infrastructure that enables organizations to safely embrace this new era.

#### **The Role**

You'll join the team designing **Okta for AI Agents** — one of Okta's newest and most critical strategic initiatives, providing identity management, access control, and governance for AI agents and non-human identities. Our customers are IT and security admins, AI developers, and system architects who need to safely deploy and govern how AI agents interact with corporate data, tools, and production environments.

As a designer on this team, you'll own key workflows end-to-end, from early-stage concepting of novel interaction models (there's no established pattern library for "agentic permissioning" yet) to hardening flows for scale as the product matures. You'll partner closely with product managers, engineers, researchers, and content designers, and work closely with our Principal and Staff designers, to turn ambiguous, highly technical agentic problems such as delegation, policy, audit, and trust boundaries between humans and agents into experiences that feel simple and safe.

#### **What you will do**

- Own key workflows end-to-end, from early-stage concepting of novel interaction models, where few established patterns exist yet, through hardening and polishing flows as the product matures and scales to millions of customers.
- Bring a systems-thinking lens to connect user insights with business goals, creating experiences that keep customers and their users and AI agents safe every day in this rapidly evolving space.
- Design experiences across multiple personas i.e Security Admins, AI Engineers, Enterprise End-users, and IT Admins, tackling complex scenarios like AI agent lifecycle management, permission scoping, human-in-the-loop authorization, and policy enforcement across the Okta portfolio.
- Contribute to scalable, usable design patterns for non-human identity and agent management and security that hold up under scale, meet a high bar for quality, and improve consistency across the product.
- Regularly present work, concepts, and new ideas to cross-functional partners and senior leadership, driving alignment and gathering input to sharpen your designs.
- Actively experiment with AI in your design process, using AI tools for brainstorming, rapid prototyping, exploring edge cases, and iterating on solutions.

#### **What we are looking for**

- You easily move between big-picture thinking and being detail-oriented.
- You're curious and have experience incorporating AI into your design process, including the ability to create shared skills and workflows, iterate on ideas collaboratively with AI, and document sophisticated prompts for scaled use.
- You evaluate AI-generated outputs for technical accuracy, design system adherence, and ease of use to serve as a necessary quality bar before designs move to production.
- You keep user goals at the heart of your solution and can also balance business objectives and engineering considerations.
- You thrive in ambiguous environments and are comfortable defining patterns, user flows and system paradigms where none exist.
- You have experience working with design systems, both in adhering to the system and contributing to an evolving design system.
- You showcase storytelling skills to communicate your design ideas to a variety of stakeholders.
- Your portfolio will show that you've worked on complex and challenging technical and have deep interest and ability to dive into complex technical subject matter + ability to translate complexity into user-understandable concepts.

#### **Qualifications**

- Degree in a related field or relevant work experience as a Product Designer or UX Designer.
- Proficient in Figma or similar design tools.
- At least a basic understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- A digital portfolio showing depth across a variety of projects, including work that shipped and was used in real products. (Portfolio link required when applying.)
- 5+ years of experience as a Product Designer.

The annual base salary range for this position for candidates located in California (excluding San Francisco Bay Area), Colorado, Illinois, New York, and Washington is between: $136,000—$187,000 USD

**The Okta Experience** — Our global community, spanning over 20 offices worldwide, is united by a drive to innovate.

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**IA como ferramenta ativa do processo de design, não como domínio do produto**

A vaga é explícita e detalhada sobre isso: "create shared skills and workflows", "document sophisticated prompts for scaled use", "evaluate AI-generated outputs for technical accuracy". É um uso instrumental e cotidiano de IA dentro do próprio fluxo de trabalho do designer. O case mais forte de IA do portfolio, Enterprise AI, é sobre desenhar a experiência de um produto de IA (rastreabilidade, transparência, confiança) — não sobre usar IA para acelerar o próprio processo de design. É uma distinção sutil mas real: a vaga não está perguntando "você já projetou algo com IA?", está perguntando "você já usa IA pra prototipar, avaliar outputs e documentar prompts no seu dia a dia?". Essa segunda pergunta não tem resposta no portfolio hoje. **Mitigação:** se Jeferson já usa ferramentas de IA (Figma AI, ChatGPT, Midjourney, etc.) no próprio fluxo de trabalho, mesmo informalmente, vale nomear isso explicitamente na candidatura — é diferente de inventar um case, é articular uma prática real que talvez não esteja documentada ainda.
