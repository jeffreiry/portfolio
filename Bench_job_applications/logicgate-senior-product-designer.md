## Job description original

LogicGate is the leading AI GRC platform for the Enterprise, helping governance, risk, and compliance teams limit surprises, strengthen resilience, augment program performance, and confidently quantify impact and business value.

**About the Role**

As a Senior Product Designer at LogicGate, you will lead the design of intuitive, high-impact user experiences across multiple features within the Risk Cloud® platform. You will navigate ambiguous opportunity spaces, partner closely with Product Management and Engineering to turn complex enterprise risk workflows into streamlined solutions.

**What You'll Do**

- Lead End-to-End Design: Own and execute complex design initiatives across multiple concurrent projects, navigating ambiguity from early discovery through final review and delivery.
- Design Complex Enterprise Experiences: Craft multi-step and data-dense user journeys—including simplifying information architecture, dashboard analytics, and AI-assisted platform interactions.
- Integrate AI Capabilities: Partner with Product and Engineering to design human-centered interactions for AI features, making complex automated risk predictions intuitive and trustworthy.
- Drive Problem Definition & Strategy: Collaborate with Product Managers to shape problem statements, break down nebulous briefs.
- Evolve the Design System: Contribute systematically to Risk Cloud's design system—proposing new components, documenting patterns.
- Execute & Accelerate Research: Lead qualitative and quantitative user research sessions and synthesis.
- Elevate Team Practice & Culture: Co-lead design critiques, facilitate process improvements, provide informal mentorship to mid-level and junior Product Designers.

**What You Bring (Required)**

- 3–7 years of experience as a Product Designer or UX Designer on complex B2B software or enterprise SaaS platforms, with a portfolio showcasing shipped, high-impact features.
- Figma & Prototyping Mastery: Advanced proficiency in Figma, component library structures, interactive prototyping, and developer handoff practices.
- Design Systems Experience: Proven track record of actively contributing to and expanding design systems at scale.
- Autonomous Problem Solving: Demonstrated ability to take ambiguous briefs, define problem spaces, and create structured, scalable solutions.
- AI Curiosity & Tool Utilization: Practical curiosity regarding AI tools and an eager mindset toward designing AI-driven product features.
- Research & Data Synthesis: Experience planning, executing, and synthesizing qualitative and quantitative user research.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration: Strong partnership skills with Product Management and Engineering.

**Nice to Have:**

- Experience designing for complex enterprise B2B workflows, data-focused solutions, or AI/automation capabilities.
- Domain familiarity with Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), cybersecurity, audit, or enterprise risk management concepts.
- Deep knowledge of web accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA) and inclusive design practices.
- Track record of contributing to internal design operations, team ceremonies, or design mentorship.

The anticipated base salary range for the role is $125,000 - $155,000 per year + variable + equity + benefits.

**Office Location** This role is classified as Hybrid, based out of our Chicago HQ or Bellevue office.

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**Mentoria formal de designers mid-level/junior**

A vaga pede "informal mentorship to mid-level and junior Product Designers" como parte das responsabilidades do cargo. Nenhum case do portfolio narra Jeferson em papel de mentoria — mesmo gap recorrente identificado em outras vagas sênior/staff do bench. Diferente dos outros requisitos desta vaga (onde o portfolio é excepcionalmente forte), este é o único ponto fraco real. **Mitigação:** como é mencionado como "informal" e não o foco central do cargo, o impacto no score geral é menor do que em vagas onde mentoria é o mandato principal — mas vale articular qualquer experiência de orientação informal de colegas, se existir.
