## Job description original

**Accountabilities:**

- Adapt existing Figma templates and visual assets to match the branding, layout, and visual identity of different online newspapers, magazines, and digital media platforms.
- Review prospect websites and identify key visual elements, including typography, colors, imagery, spacing, composition, and overall design language.
- Create high-fidelity digital mockups demonstrating how branded content and advertising experiences could appear within online publications.
- Update typography, color palettes, layouts, visual framing, and other design elements across pitch materials and presentation assets.
- Prepare visual assets and video frames used in customized sales presentations and digital publishing concepts.
- Make occasional updates to presentation decks, digital collateral, and other visual materials based on project needs.
- Collaborate closely with sales and production teams to deliver polished, accurate materials within short deadlines.
- Manage multiple short, project-based assignments independently while maintaining consistency and attention to detail across deliverables.

**Requirements**

- Hands-on professional experience with Figma and strong knowledge of its layout and design capabilities.
- Strong visual design skills, particularly in layout, typography, composition, color, and visual hierarchy.
- Excellent attention to detail and the ability to accurately adapt visual assets to different brand identities and design systems.
- Experience creating digital marketing materials, presentations, branded content, visual assets, or similar creative deliverables.
- Ability to interpret an existing visual direction and translate it into polished, consistent, high-fidelity assets.
- Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to work independently and move efficiently through short, project-based assignments.
- Advanced English proficiency, with the ability to communicate clearly and effectively in a professional environment.
- Based in Latin America, with availability for the role's working hours.
