## Job description original

**Who We Are**

At Fidelity, we've been helping Canadian investors build better financial futures for over 35 years. We offer individuals and institutions a range of trusted investment portfolios and services.

**What You Will Do**

The Software Product Designer is responsible for user-centric-driven product design to deliver best of class digital experiences to end users in a new suite of software applications. This includes working with the product and marketing teams to develop target user personae; consistent investment in brand and design systems; working with product management and development teams on rapid prototyping of user experience; validation of product through user testing; and using design thinking to develop deep user insights to drive future digital product innovation.

**The Value You Deliver**

- Advocate for the user experience (UX), articulated through the development of user personae, maps and journeys, and prototypes.
- Work with Marketing and Development team to develop consistent digital brand and accompanying design system artifacts.
- Develop prototypes and conduct user research to validate designs and identify gaps in the user experience.
- Use deep customer insights as a feedback loop for future product strategy and features design.

**What We're Looking For**

- Experience with IOS and Android mobile application development.
- Experience building design systems in design tools such as Sketch or Adobe Suite.
- Experience with prototyping tools such as InVision, Figma, or Axure.
- Knowledge and experience in both data-based and interview-based product design and development methodologies.
- Knowledge and experience in design thinking and validation techniques such as Sprint methodology.
- Ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive consensus on user experience.
- Experience running collaborative design thinking workshops.
- Understanding of digital and Canadian accessibility standards.

**Experience, Education**

- Completion of a College degree, preferably in a Computer Science, Engineering, Industrial Design, Kinesiology or HCI related field or equivalent work experience.
- 3+ years in Software Application Design.
- Experience in brand development or marketing and communications is an asset.

Total compensation: $101,000 to $118,000 base + discretionary bonus. Work location: Toronto (hybrid).
