**About the Team**

We are Quadcode, a fintech company excelling in financial brokerage activities and delivering advanced financial products to our global clientele. Our flagship product, an internal trading platform, is offered as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) solution to other brokers. We are seeking an experienced Product Designer to join and strengthen our team. You will work closely with fellow Designers, a Design Technologist, Product Owner, Researcher, and the Development team.

**Key Requirements**

- 5+ years of experience in product design with a portfolio featuring launched case studies.
- Advanced proficiency in Figma.
- Proven experience in creating or scaling design systems.
- A product mindset based on data, research, and common sense with understanding of how design impacts business metrics.
- Understanding of accessibility principles.
- Strong communication skills with the ability to articulate and defend design decisions.
- Self-management capabilities, comfort with shifting priorities based on business needs.

**Main Responsibilities**

- Owning the design and user experience for key product areas.
- Designing and improving user scenarios across Web, iOS, and Android platforms.
- Conducting product discovery.
- Building high-quality prototypes.
- Facilitating discussions with developers, ensuring implementation quality.
- Contributing to the design system.

Remote work model, competitive remuneration.
