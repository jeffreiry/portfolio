## Job description original

Gong harnesses the power of AI to transform how revenue teams win. The Gong Revenue AI Operating System unifies data, insights, and workflows into a single, trusted system that observes, guides, and acts alongside the world's most successful revenue teams. Powered by the Gong Revenue Graph, AI-powered intelligence, specialized agents, and trusted applications, Gong helps more than 5,000 companies around the world deeply understand their teams and customers, automate critical sales workflows, and close more deals with less effort.

We're seeking a highly motivated Senior Product Designer to play a key role in designing, exploring, and continually delivering innovative solutions and experiences.

**You'll Own:**

- Drive the user experience and design for one of Gong's key product areas.
- Lead end-to-end design processes — from ideation and wireframes to polished, high-fidelity designs.
- Champion user-centered design principles and act as the voice of the user across the product lifecycle.
- Help shape and refine Gong's UI pattern libraries and design guidelines.
- Research and translate complex B2B SaaS user problems and workflows into intuitive solutions.
- Collaborate closely with Product Managers and Engineers, using prototyping and usability testing to validate direction.

**What You'll Bring:**

- 8+ years of experience designing flows, experiences, and UI for web and mobile products, preferably in B2B SaaS.
- Strong rapid and high-fidelity prototyping skills.
- Ability to create clear, intuitive, user-friendly designs.
- Creative, self-driven mindset with strong problem-solving abilities.
- Experience with user research and usability testing.
- Proficiency with Figma and modern design tools.
- Strong collaboration and communication skills.
- Fluent written and verbal English.
- A portfolio of recent, relevant work.

**Nice to Have:** Degree in Design, Human Factors Engineering, or a related psychology field.

**Location:** Hybrid, Tel Aviv, Israel. Gong operates on a 5-day work week with flexible vacation and quarterly recharge days.
