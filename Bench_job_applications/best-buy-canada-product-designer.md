## Job description original

Salary: $85,000 - 110,000 per year

**Requirements**

We are looking for a product designer with 5 years of experience spanning strategy, research, interaction and UI design, and validation. We need someone with experience creating end-to-end experiences across multiple touchpoints, including digital and physical channels. We value the ability to quickly get up to speed on active work and deliver within agreed timelines. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills across all organizational levels.

Best Buy Canada is seeking experienced Product Designers to join their One Best Buy Pursuit Group, focusing on enhancing digital and in-store customer experiences. The positions include Product Designer II (permanent) and Product Designer III (12-month fixed-term) roles located in Vancouver, BC with a remote-first model.

Required skills include being comfortable facilitating discussions and driving alignment through ambiguity. The role involves providing senior-level design leadership for ongoing digital and in-store initiatives.

**Preferred skills:**

- Exposure to and knowledge of best practices for AI-enabled design tools.
- Experience with or strong understanding of design systems, including their creation, maintenance, and application.
- Familiarity with accessibility standards (e.g., WCAG) and their integration into the design process.
