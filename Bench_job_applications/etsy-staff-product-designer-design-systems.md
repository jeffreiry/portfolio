## Job description original

Etsy is the global marketplace for unique and creative goods. Collage is Etsy's design system, and it's how quality ships at scale. Every buyer and seller surface across web, iOS, Android, and Flutter is built from it. As a Staff Product Designer on the Design Systems team within UX Platform, you will help drive growth and architecture of that system.

This role sits at a specific moment. Collage has mature design language and token foundations, and much of the component library is built. The next phase is harder and more interesting: making the system opinionated rather than reactive, working with consumers to define pattern libraries, closing the gaps in how components are proposed, built, graduated, and retired.

**What does the day-to-day look like?**

- Architect the system: refine component anatomy, API and composition models, variant strategies, token structures. Make decisions that hold across web, iOS, Android, and Flutter.
- Refine the component lifecycle: define how a component gets proposed, prioritized, designed, documented, graduated, and deprecated.
- Make the system operable at scale: use agentic tools like Cursor or Claude in your daily practice to prototype architecture, validate decisions.
- Work in the open: document decisions as you make them.
- Drive adoption through craft: use hands-on partnership with teams consuming Collage.
- Partner deeply with engineering: work day to day with web and native engineering leads.
- Set and hold the quality bar: champion accessibility, precision, completeness.

**Qualities that will help you thrive:**

- A portfolio showing design systems work at scale: component architecture, token systems, and delivery across web, iOS, and Android.
- Judgment about where the system should be opinionated and where it should stay flexible.
- Comfort flexing how you work to reach a quality result, using agentic tools like Cursor or Claude.
- A transparent way of working. You write things down, share reasoning.
- Genuine comfort working with engineers. You can read a component implementation.
- Strong visual craft and a rigorous eye for typography, color, space, and density as system-level decisions.
- The ability to operate in ambiguity, frame a poorly-defined problem, align stakeholders.
- Excellent communication and storytelling.

**As a Staff Designer, you will be expected to:**

- Lead cross-squad design objectives and provide strategic leadership.
- Set visual and interaction standards that resolve complex problems across multiple surfaces and platforms.
- Contribute strategic improvements to the design system.
- Help designers across the org adapt their work.
- Build prototypes that clarify direction and create organizational alignment.
- Model giving and applying feedback in a way that raises the quality of work around you.

**Salary Range:** Geo-1: 187,000.00 - 253,000.00 USD Annual | Geo-2: 168,000.00 - 228,000.00 USD Annual

We prioritize candidates based within commutable distance of Etsy's Brooklyn Office.

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**Escala multi-plataforma (web, iOS, Android, Flutter) ausente**

A vaga é explícita: decisões de Design System precisam "hold across web, iOS, Android, and Flutter" — quatro plataformas, duas delas mobile nativo e uma framework cross-platform específico. O portfolio de Jeferson não tem nenhum Design System que cubra mobile nativo; Power Apps Dummy App é single-platform (low-code Microsoft). Esse é um gap estrutural de escala, não de competência de design em si.

**Liderança estratégica cross-squad**

"Lead cross-squad design objectives and provide strategic leadership" é uma expectativa central e explícita de nível Staff aqui — mais pesada que em outras vagas Staff analisadas hoje. Todo o portfolio é trabalho de individual contributor; não há relato de liderança formal de múltiplos squads ou iniciativas estratégicas acima do nível de projeto único.

**Ferramentas agênticas de IA no fluxo de trabalho**

A vaga pede uso concreto de "Cursor or Claude" na prática diária pra prototipar arquitetura — ausente no portfolio, mesmo gap recorrente de outras vagas que pedem IA como ferramenta de processo.
