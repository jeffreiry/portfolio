## Job description original

Greenlight is a family technology company on a mission to help families raise financially smart kids. Its suite of products — including the Greenlight app, debit card, Safe Family GPS trackers, and Family Hub — brings together financial tools, safety features, and everyday family management. Today, more than 6.5 million family members trust Greenlight.

We are looking for a Senior Product Designer with a passion for creating great products for great people (in our case the modern family). You will partner closely with both our IDC office (Bangalore) and the US based design team, contributing to UX/UI direction.

**What you will be doing:**

- Work closely with Product and Engineering based in IDC.
- Collaborate with other UX/UI designers to ensure alignment across the product and design system.
- Work with developers to ensure designs are implemented accurately.
- Create key UI views, UX flows, and components.
- Conduct quality assurance checks to ensure consistency and functionality of design elements across different devices and platforms.

**What you should bring:**

- 5-7 years + proven experience as a Product Designer or similar role with a strong portfolio showcasing your skills and attention to detail, preferably for a mobile application.
- Mastery and speed in Figma, with ability to produce prototypes to support engineers.
- Knowledge and efficiency of Adobe Creative Suite.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
- Eye for detail, consistency, and a passion for creating high-quality, user-centered designs.
- A portfolio showcasing your work.
- Comprehensive knowledge of standard iOS and Android mobile design patterns.
