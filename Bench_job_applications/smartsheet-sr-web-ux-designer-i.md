## Job description original

For over 20 years, Smartsheet has empowered teams to manage work seamlessly and scale solutions smarter. Now, in our most ambitious chapter yet, we are uniting human teams with AI agents.

The Web UX Design team, part of Digital Demand Marketing, is looking for a Senior Web UX Designer to design engaging, valuable, and intuitive experiences for our website. This role is design-focused, with a strong emphasis on translating ideas into buildable, well documented components for a fully asynchronous, globally distributed engineering team. You'll help maintain our Web design system components, and you'll see your work through from concept to launch, working hands-on with developers through Jira, redlines, and branch review.

This role is eligible for remote work within Costa Rica. You must reside in Costa Rica.

**You Will:**

- Design user journeys, wireframes, and high fidelity prototypes for web pages and components, applying and extending our existing web design system.
- Help boost design quality and system maturity by contributing components and patterns to our web design system.
- Prepare clear specifications, redlines, and handoff documentation for a fully asynchronous, globally distributed engineering team.
- Work directly in Jira alongside developers, tracking design tickets through to completion.
- Review live branches and staging builds against design specs, flagging mismatches before launch.
- Conduct user research in collaboration with our research lead.
- Use AI assisted and design-to-code tools to speed up handoff and reduce manual production work.
- Collaborate with engineers, web authors, TPMs, and web strategists to ship polished, accessible experiences.

**You have:**

- 5+ years of experience designing web or product experiences, ideally within a CMS driven or component based environment.
- Experience preparing detailed specs and redlines for engineering handoff, particularly in an asynchronous or distributed team setting.
- Comfort working directly in Jira or a similar ticketing system alongside a development team.
- Familiarity with design systems, tokens, and component libraries.
- Understanding of accessibility (WCAG) standards.
- Strong visual design skills, with an eye for detail and consistency.
- Applied understanding of core design principles: hierarchy, typography, color, and layout.
- Strong interaction design and prototyping skills.
- High degree of proficiency with Figma.
- Exposure to usability testing, user research, or working from research findings.
- Basic understanding of HTML and CSS, and curiosity about how designs get built.
- Experience leveraging AI tools in your daily design workflow.
- Degree in Design or a related field, or equivalent professional experience.
- A portfolio that shows strong UI/UX craft.

**Nice to have:**

- Adobe design tools (Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop).
- Experience with motion (Adobe After Effects, Lottie).
- Familiarity with front end development concepts or a front end coding background.
- Experience with AI assisted and generative design tools, such as Figma Make, Lovable, or similar.
