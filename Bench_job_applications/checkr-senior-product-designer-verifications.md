## Job description original

Checkr is building the data platform to power safe and fair decisions. Over 140,000 companies and millions of people rely on Checkr for AI verification in the moments that matter most. Customers include Uber, Pennymac, Airbnb, Doordash, and Anthropic.

**About the role**

We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to help shape the end-to-end experiences that power Checkr's Verifications products—spanning employment, education, criminal record, and driving record verifications, with a key focus on drug & occupational health testing. You'll partner closely with Product, Engineering, and cross-functional stakeholders to design intuitive workflows for complex, high-trust domains.

**What you'll do**

- Own design for key Verifications product areas—including drug & occupational health testing—from discovery through launch and iteration.
- Partner with Product and Engineering to define problems, shape roadmaps, and deliver high-quality solutions.
- Simplify complex workflows (verification requests, document collection, consent/authorization, appointment scheduling, status visibility, exception handling) into clear, user-friendly experiences.
- Use AI-assisted design and development tools to rapidly prototype, validate, and implement lightweight UI changes in code.
- Design for trust and clarity in a sensitive, regulated domain—prioritizing transparency, error prevention, accessibility.
- Translate insights into outcomes by combining qualitative research, stakeholder input, and product data.
- Create thoughtful interaction and visual design across responsive web and/or platform surfaces.
- Facilitate alignment with workshops, design reviews, and strong storytelling.
- Collaborate with Customer Success, Sales, Compliance/Legal, Data, and Operations.
- Contribute to design systems and standards.

**What you bring**

- 5–8+ years of product design experience (or equivalent), with a strong track record of shipping high-quality work.
- Portfolio demonstrates end-to-end thinking: problem framing, exploration, rationale, collaboration, iteration, and measurable impact.
- Comfortable working in complex domains where edge cases matter.
- Strong product instincts—balance user needs, business goals, technical constraints, and timeline realities.
- Experienced in collaborative, cross-functional environments, influencing decisions through clarity rather than authority.
- Can independently plan and execute lightweight research (interviews, usability testing, concept validation).
- Sweat the details: interaction patterns, content clarity, information hierarchy, and accessibility best practices.
- An A-player mindset with a strong bias for action.

**Nice to have**

- Experience in background screening, HR tech, healthcare, identity, compliance-heavy, or other high-trust regulated spaces.
- Experience designing B2B / B2B2C products, especially workflows used by operations teams or external partners.
- Familiarity with design systems, documentation, and scalable component thinking.
- Experience designing for secure data, consent, and privacy-forward user experiences.

On-target Earnings OR Base Salary range (San Francisco, CA): $169,000—$199,000 USD
On-target Earnings OR Base Salary range (Denver, CO): $143,000—$168,000 USD

At Checkr, we believe an in office work environment strengthens collaboration. Our hub locations are Denver, CO; San Francisco, CA; Nashville, TN; and Santiago, Chile. Individuals are expected to work from the office 3+ days a week.
