## Job description original

Headquarters: Remote, United States

About Calm: Calm is the leading mental health brand on a mission to make the world happier and healthier. Known for its flagship consumer app—ranked #1 in its category with over 180 million downloads and availability in ten languages across 190 countries—Calm helps people sleep better, stress less, and live more mindfully.

As a Senior Product Designer on our Consumer team, you'll shape experiences used by tens of millions of people to sleep better, stress less, and live more mindfully. People often reach for Calm in their most vulnerable moments, so the craft of what we make (how it looks, moves, and feels) matters enormously.

This is a builder's role in the truest sense: you'll use AI and modern prototyping tools to move from insight to interactive prototype to shipped experience, not as a shortcut on quality, but to spend less time producing and more time on the judgment and detail that make an experience great.

**What You'll Do**

- Own end-to-end product design initiatives that cover the whole product experience (visual, interaction, motion, brand integration), to create thoughtful, high-quality user-centered mobile experiences.
- Prototype quickly in Figma and with AI and low-code tools like Replit, Lovable, and Claude, so you can get real, testable ideas in front of people early.
- Present your design work clearly and persuasively to cross-functional stakeholders (including senior leadership).
- Maintain strong attention to detail and collaboration through engineering implementation (build and QA) and post launch-iterations.
- Use data and drive research to inform the experiences you build.
- Champion system-level thinking (improving how the team works and evolving Calm's design system).
- Balance multiple projects in a fast-paced environment while maintaining high standards of execution.

**Who You Are**

- Experienced Product Designer: 7+ years of experience in UX and UI design with 5+ within consumer mobile and web applications.
- AI-native and tooling-fluent: Highly proficient in Figma for design, and already using AI and low-code tools (Replit, Lovable, Claude) to prototype and move fast.
- Technical agility: Comfortable shipping small code changes without engineering support.
- A builder: Biased toward shipping and iterating over perfect plans.
- Data- and research-informed: You fold both qualitative and quantitative insight into your design decisions.
- A strong collaborator: A low-ego, trusted partner to Product, Research, and Engineering.
- High craft standards: A strong advocate for accessibility, consistency, visual storytelling, brand integration, and motion design.
- A clear communicator: You present your thinking persuasively to stakeholders and senior leadership.

**Nice to Haves**

- Experience with conversational or AI-native UX.
- Experience working at multiple organizations, ideally including time at a consumer product in a fast-paced startup environment.
- A strong portfolio demonstrating your ability to design and ship beautiful, functional, and user-friendly experiences.
- Comfort shipping small copy and UI changes directly in code, without engineering support.
- A passion for Calm's mission and a point of view on how digital products can support mental health.

**Minimum Requirements**

- 7+ years in UX/UI design with 5+ within consumer mobile (and web) applications.
- Proven experience driving user testing and research.
- Strong foundation in interaction design, visual design, and usability best practices.
- Experience designing and shipping mobile applications at scale.
- Hands-on proficiency in Figma and modern AI/prototyping tools.
- Ability to work independently while contributing to a larger design vision and strategy.

Calm uses a geographic pay model. Base pay ranges across Calm's pay tiers: $168,000 - $245,000.

## Gaps prioritários

### 🔴 Bloqueadores de candidatura

**Ausência de experiência consumer mobile em escala**

A vaga exige explicitamente "5+ anos em aplicações mobile e web consumer" e menciona a escala real da Calm (180 milhões de downloads) como contexto operacional direto do cargo. O portfolio de Jeferson é inteiramente B2B enterprise ou projetos de marca pontuais — nenhum produto consumer de massa, nenhum app mobile nativo. Essa combinação (domínio consumer + mobile + escala) é o núcleo da vaga, não um detalhe periférico, o que torna este provavelmente o bloqueador mais difícil de mitigar com argumento de transferência. **Mitigação:** o argumento mais honesto disponível é a decisão de design responsivo do Arezzo, mas isso não se aproxima da exigência real de "shipping mobile applications at scale".

**IA como ferramenta de prototipação rápida, não como domínio do produto**

A vaga é extremamente específica: "using AI and modern prototyping tools to move from insight to interactive prototype to shipped experience" e nomeia ferramentas concretas (Replit, Lovable, Claude). Mesmo padrão de gap já identificado em outras vagas do bench — Enterprise AI é sobre desenhar um produto de IA, não sobre usar IA para acelerar o processo pessoal de prototipação. **Mitigação:** se Jeferson já usa essas ou ferramentas similares no dia a dia, nomear isso explicitamente é a única forma de fechar esse gap sem inventar experiência.
